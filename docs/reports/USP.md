# EquiLens AI – Unique Selling Proposition & Product Differentiation

> **Tagline:** *See Beyond the Stock Price.*

EquiLens AI bridges the gap between conventional quantitative stock screeners and modern artificial intelligence by delivering an evidence-grounded financial intelligence terminal. This document outlines the core value proposition, target users, market friction solved, competitive positioning, and the factual implementation status of its 10 core differentiators.

---

## 1. Core Value Proposition

Modern equity research is crippled by two extremes:
1. **Traditional Screeners (Screener.in, Trendlyne, Yahoo Finance):** Excel at displaying historical numbers and static multiples, but provide zero capability to query management commentary, strategic capital shifts, or risk factors buried in 300-page annual reports.
2. **Generic LLM Chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity):** Excel at conversational fluency, but hallucinate accounting numbers, invent non-existent filings, fail at mathematical formulas, and produce unverified financial citations.

**EquiLens AI's Core Value Proposition:**  
A unified terminal where **unstructured document intelligence (RAG)** is deterministically bound to **verified quantitative accounting formulas** and **econometric forecasting models**, providing verifiable page-level citations for every factual statement.

## 3. The 10 Core Product Differentiators

Below is an empirical analysis of EquiLens AI's 10 proposed differentiators, distinguishing **Implemented Capabilities** from **Planned / Staged Capabilities**:

### 1. Unified Financial Intelligence
* **Value:** Integrates financial statements, stock prices, news, filings, forecasting, and corporate actions into a single coherent interface.
* **Status:** **Implemented.** Built with unified PostgreSQL schemas, cross-referenced company UUIDs, and a responsive React terminal layout.

### 2. Document-Grounded AI Research with Page-Level Citations
* **Value:** Every factual statement generated by the AI assistant links back to the exact document, section, and page number of an authentic filing.
* **Status:** **Implemented.** The `CitationEngine` extracts verified page numbers, document titles, and source excerpts into typed `Citation` records.

### 3. Company-Specific Financial Analysis
* **Value:** Deterministic calculation of fundamental ratios (ROE, ROCE, FCF, Margins, Debt-to-Equity) in Python, completely bypassing LLM arithmetic.
* **Status:** **Implemented.** Executed via `FinancialCalculator` with strict division-by-zero safeguards and unit consistency (INR Crores).

### 4. Historical & Future Performance Forecasting
* **Value:** Econometric projections (Revenue, EBITDA, PAT) across Base, Bullish (+15-20%), and Bearish (-15%) scenarios with 80% and 95% parametric confidence bounds.
* **Status:** **Implemented.** Backed by walk-forward cross-validation metrics (MAE, RMSE, MAPE) and explicit compliance disclaimers.

### 5. News-to-Stock-Impact Intelligence
* **Value:** Real-time ingestion of Indian market news with collision-safe regex entity matching and tone sentiment scoring.
* **Status:** **Implemented.** Handled by `NewsService` using the Marketaux REST API and curated announcement fixtures.

### 6. Corporate Action Intelligence
* **Value:** Detailed tracking of dividends, face-value stock splits, bonus share issuances, and buybacks with record and ex-dates.
* **Status:** **Implemented.** Captured in the `corporate_actions` database table and rendered on dedicated timeline cards.

### 7. Major Deal & Contract Analysis
* **Value:** Extraction of material commercial developments, deal values in INR, counterparties, and strategic business implications.
* **Status:** **Implemented.** Captured in the `market_events` table and exposed via `/api/v1/market-events`.

### 8. Integrated RAG and Quantitative Analytics
* **Value:** RAG synthesis prompts receive both retrieved unstructured filing chunks AND deterministically computed financial ratios.
* **Status:** **Implemented.** Prompt assembly combines qualitative disclosures with verified mathematical metrics.

### 9. Multi-Source Financial Research
* **Value:** Simultaneously processes regulatory filings (PDF), earnings call transcripts (DOCX), financial models (XLSX), news feeds (Marketaux), and broker feeds (DhanHQ).
* **Status:** **Partially Implemented.** Multi-format loaders (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV) and news feeds are active; real-time DhanHQ market data feed operates with verified NSE/BSE fixtures.

### 10. Explainable AI-Generated Research Reports
* **Value:** Automated generation of institutional equity research briefs with executive summaries, risk analysis, and multi-scenario outlooks.
* **Status:** **Implemented.** Generated via Gemini 3.5 Flash and rendered in `ResearchReportsPage.tsx`.

## 5. Architectural Advantages

1. **Deterministic Separation of Responsibilities:** Calculations belong in code; qualitative interpretation belongs in LLMs. EquiLens AI never asks an LLM to multiply or divide financial statements.
2. **Graceful Engine Degradation:** Operating with an explicit `EngineRegistry` allows the platform to function continuously even if a third-party API (e.g., market data or news) hits rate limits.
3. **Institutional Security & Data Isolation:** Private files are stored in access-controlled buckets; pgvector similarity queries are strictly filtered by company and user permissions.
4. **Transparent Telemetry:** Full visibility into token usage, model fallbacks, and inference latencies via Langfuse and live API integration audits.
